Get Your Ham Radio License

Ready to get on the air? These resources can help you learn the material, practice for your exam, and find a license class in the region.

Most new operators begin with the Technician license. Choose a class or study at your own pace, then use the practice tests below to check your progress.

Practice Tests

Take practice exams until you are comfortable with the question pool and your scores are consistent.

  • QRZ Ham Test
  • HamExam.org
  • HamStudy.org

Training Classes

Instructor-led classes can provide a schedule, a study group, and a place to ask questions.

  • West Seattle Amateur Radio Club (W7AW) classes
  • Mike and Key Amateur Radio Club (K7LED), Renton classes
  • Radio Club of Tacoma (W7DK) Technician resources

After You Pass

Once you pass your exam, keep learning by attending a HARC meeting, joining a local net, and connecting with other operators. See our meetings and frequencies pages to get started.
