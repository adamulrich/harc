McMicken Water Tower

Progress Summary

This project is focused on preparing the McMicken site for a new HARC container installation. The main site work under review is the concrete pad expansion needed to support the new enclosure footprint. The existing enclosure is approximately 6 feet by 6 feet 4 inches, and the existing pad is about 7 feet 8 inches wide by 7 feet 6 inches long, including the threshold on the south end. The HARC building footprint is about 7 feet 4 inches wide by 8 feet long, and the current pad requirement under review is 8 feet 8 inches wide by 8 feet 4 inches long, again with the threshold at the south end. Based on the current design notes, that means the pour would extend the existing pad by about 2 feet in width and 18 inches in length.
